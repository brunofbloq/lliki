Review the legacy project-context files detected in this repository and propose a safe migration into `wiki/`.

1. Read `wiki/index.md` and `wiki/wiki-rules.md`.
2. Inspect only the detected legacy context directories and files.
3. Map useful content into tasks, decisions, lessons, exploratory reports, or maintained documentation according to the wiki rules.
4. Identify duplicates, contradictions, obsolete content, and unresolved information.
5. Do not delete, move, or overwrite legacy files.
6. Present a migration plan with source, destination, content to preserve, content to omit or archive, and conflicts requiring human review.
7. Keep `wiki/index.md` concise and use it only as a routing entry point.
